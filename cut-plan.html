Parts list

The middle-bay hatch panel takes a 60 mm bored hole on the centreline, 245 mm forward of the bay centre, on the rear/mid bed-panel joint — the bedside post drops through it so the table can be fitted with the hatch in place. Line the hole with a steel rim.

Table joint fittings, per pair of halves: two 8 mm × 60 mm hardwood dowels in the mating edges (30 mm each side, 200 mm apart), two over-centre toggle latches on the underside 110 mm apart, and two 30 mm table posts with floor and top plates. No hinge — the halves separate, so the surface stays flat.

D1a/D1b are listed at their rectangular blank size; both are cut to the teardrop plan profile. Set out the full 1360 mm top as one shape — a 100 mm radius nose at the outboard end and a 190 mm radius tail at the other, joined by the straight tangent between the two arcs — then split it at the 680 mm line on a curve rather than a straight cut: a shallow 55 mm bow, convex on D1a and concave on D1b, running out to nothing at both ends of the joint. Round all edges over with a 6 mm radius. Neither half has a straight edge.

Bed panels are listed as solid boards. The model draws them slatted; if you cut them that way, substitute two 90 mm rails plus slats per panel and the sheet count drops by about half a sheet. The four drawer fronts are 22 mm faced board in the model — they are nested here on the 18 mm sheets for convenience, so cut them from their own material and treat that nest space as spare. Nesting is a guillotine shelf fit with the kerf allowed between parts, rotation permitted — grain direction is not constrained, so re-orient any part where face grain matters before cutting.
