Key features

myCampal box for the Ford Tourneo Custom — 1200 × 715 × 590 mm, floor-mounted on two cleats.

Bed, 2000 mm
Panels run out over the drawers and land on the cab-end support bar. Side wings fold down to 1550 mm across. Sliding and folding mechanisms both modelled.
Lounger
The forward panel lifts on a ply prop into a backrest, with the overlay board staying flat beneath it.
Kitchen drawer
Twin-burner stove on the upper deck with a windshield, a fold-down side table front, and a side-opening tray at full extension.
Storage crate
Deep open crate with hand-hole cut-outs, 240 mm high.
Prep drawer
Two lift-off ply lids: chopping boards one way up, steel trivet bars on the reverse.
Dual-access fridge
Compressor fridge centred in its drawer with a latched front at each end, so it runs out off the tailgate or forward into the load bay.
Middle-bay shelf
Removable hatch front that seats at two heights, with the bay open front and back so gear passes through from the cab.
Tables
Two-half pedestal table on posts for the seating bay, plus a single-post bedside table that clips flat to the cab-end face when stowed.
Travel mode
Every panel folds flat with the wings nesting and the legs stowing into the stack, level with the deck.
